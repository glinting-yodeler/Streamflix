StreamFlix Architecture

1. Producer generates synthetic OTT events.
2. Redpanda acts as Kafka-compatible streaming broker.
3. Consumer reads topic streamflix_events and writes raw_events to PostgreSQL.
4. Airflow DAG runs scheduled data tasks:
   - seed_dimensions
   - clean_events
   - build_marts
   - train_churn_model
   - generate_recommendations
   - generate_executive_summary
5. Streamlit reads PostgreSQL analytics tables and displays dashboards.
